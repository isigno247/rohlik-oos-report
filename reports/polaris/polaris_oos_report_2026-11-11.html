Method

Each of the 5 tracked zones (Chrášťany, Praha Liboc, Horní Počernice, Brno, Ostrava) was measured in its own fresh session after selecting the zone's delivery address, and verified before any number was used: every stock record had to come from the warehouse Rohlik assigned to that address, and no two zones could resolve to the same warehouse. All 5 zones verified successfully this run; no zone failed verification.

Listing status (in stock / out of stock / not listed) comes from a stored footprint baseline — established 2026-09-21 from a user-supplied per-zone carried/not-carried map for the 14 tracked SKUs, cross-checked against live measurement at the time. The footprint is refreshed only on request, not automatically; it was not changed by this run. Today's run measured only unit counts and prices against that stored baseline — it did not re-derive listing status.

No positions were unverified this run (every SKU/zone combination has a footprint entry).

The low-stock threshold is fixed at 5 units or fewer. Prices and promotion status are reported per individual warehouse and are never averaged or collapsed into a range. No SKU showed a promotional (struck-through) price in any zone during this run.

This report is a point-in-time snapshot, not a live feed — stock levels can and do change within minutes of measurement.
